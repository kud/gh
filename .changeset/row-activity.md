Verbs can run on the row. An `InboxExtension` with `run` keeps the list live while it works: `target.activity` draws a spinner, a confirm prompt, a nudge or a trailing word on the row, and `merged(url)` plays the merged hold. ↵ on a row with a run opens the verb's own view; a second verb on a busy row is refused; `q` asks once before quitting mid-run.
