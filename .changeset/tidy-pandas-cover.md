Your own open pull requests now all arrive, however many you have. The personal search used to stop at 30 rows, so when another organisation filled that window your view quietly showed only part of your list. It now asks for 100 at a time and keeps fetching until it has seen them all, stopping after five pages. If you ever pass that, the tab still says honestly how many it is showing out of how many there are. Fetching through the new `fetchInbox` is what turns this on; building the queries by hand works exactly as before.
