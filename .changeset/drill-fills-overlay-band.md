A PR's Conversation tab drew its first author rule over an empty pane, with the scroll indicator reading `1–1 / 53`. Inside the inbox shell the drill's body box hugged its content instead of filling the band the shell gives it, and the conversation's scroll view, which starts one row tall and grows only into room its parent offers, measured one row and stayed there. The drill body now fills the band, so the conversation, and any other scrolling drill such as a check's log, uses the whole height.
