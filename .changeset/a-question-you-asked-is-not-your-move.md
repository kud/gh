A thread you opened on somebody else's PR is theirs until they answer.

`threads` fires on any unresolved thread, PR-wide and author-blind, and from a PR you had already reviewed the `YOURS` table claimed it unconditionally — so a review comment of your own that nobody had replied to sat under Your move while the turn arrow on the same row read `→`, you spoke last. The claim is now a conjunction: from `spoken`, `threads` is yours only while somebody else has the last word, an arm beside the `authored` one it mirrors. When the login is unknown it reads as theirs, the file's own bias for a search that is provably not waiting on you. From `queued` nothing changes — the review is the claim there, and `computeHealth` ranks `threads` above `waiting`, so a re-requested review with your own unanswered thread stays yours.

On the way: `filterByOrigin`, `filterBySearch` and `filterByRepos` re-lay the kept rows out and took no `login`, so every band computed after typing a search or toggling a filter threw `lastActor` away — a row claimed by somebody's last word fell from Your move to Their move the moment `/` was pressed. They take an optional `login` now and the inbox passes it.
