Cockpit gets out of your way sooner after you act. A merged row sparkles for four turns instead of five (2.4 s), a closed row leaves after 1.5 s instead of 2.5 s, and confirmations in the inbox footer and the health panel clear after 1.5 s, so the key hints come back faster. The refresh mark on work done elsewhere keeps its 7 s: that one reports news rather than answering a keypress.

A second confirmation no longer disappears early. Both flashes now clear the previous timer, where before the first message's timer wiped whatever had replaced it.
